# 42–50" TV panel-by-size report (2025/2026 model years, EU/Norway)

Compiled 2026-11-26. Confidence: **H** = official spec page or RTINGS measurement text; **M** = one strong independent review, or several that agree; **L** = conflicting sources, retailer/forum info, or my own inference.

**Access limits.** curl could reach rtings.com, lg.com/uk, samsung.com/ie and documents.philips.com. WebFetch could not resolve rtings, displayspecifications or tftcentral. displayspecifications.com returned a bot wall to curl. whathifi, flatpanelshd, techradar, tomsguide, avforums, expertreviews and notebookcheck could not be reached, so claims from those sites come from WebSearch result snippets only. RTINGS pages download, but the numeric test values are paywalled ("Lock cd/m²"). ## Key findings (what changes the score)

1. **2025 LG 48" panels are the weak point.** The C5 42/48 have **no Brightness Booster**; LG's own footnote excludes "OLED evo C5 48" & 42"" [lg.com/uk OLED48C54LA, H]. The **G5 48" is not Primary RGB Tandem**. It is a conventional "OLED evo" panel with **"Brightness Booster Max", 1.5x vs B5 @10%**, and **VRR 144 Hz instead of 165 Hz** [lg.com/uk OLED48G56LS, H; What Hi-Fi/Expert Reviews via search, M]. The G5 55–83 have the tandem panel with Booster Ultimate.
2. **2026 is different: a 48" LG Display tandem panel exists.** RTINGS bought and tested the **LG C6 48**. It says the 48" "uses a more colorful Tandem OLED panel, similar to the LG C6H", and that it has Brightness Booster [rtings monitor/lg/c6-48-oled, H for the measured panel]. The **C6 42/55/65 are ordinary WOLED** (RTINGS C6 review, H). That makes **C6 48 a better panel than C6 55**. LG UK confirms that "Brightness Booster applies to 65/55/48-inch OLED C6", so the 42" does not get it [lg.com/uk, H]. Pre-launch press said "C6 48 = WOLED", which conflicts with this. The measured RTINGS result wins.
3. **The same 48" tandem panel appears to be used by other brands:**
   - **Samsung S90H 48.** RTINGS: "the 48-inch and 77-inch models use an RGB tandem panel". The 77S90H review says its results "are valid for … the 48-inch model" [H for US units].
   - **Philips 48OLED911.** The official leaflet says "Bright Tandem WOLED panel", 120 Hz native, 165 Hz VRR [documents.philips.com, H].
   - **LG G6 48.** LG UK: "Brightness Booster Pro, X2.1 brighter … applies to the 48-inch OLED G6", vs **Booster Ultra X3.9** and "Reflection Free **Premium**" on 55–83. The 48" gets only "Reflection Free" [lg.com/uk OLED48G66LS/OLED55G66LS, H]. TFTCentral says the G6 48 does not get the Primary RGB Tandem **2.0** panel [via search, M]. It is most likely the same 48" tandem panel as the C6 48 [L].
   - **Samsung S95H 48 (QE48S95HHEXXU).** Panel maker is LG Display (displayspecifications via search, M). It is not QD-OLED, because Samsung Display makes no 48" QD-OLED [Counterpoint/What Hi-Fi via search, M]. Whether it is the tandem panel is unconfirmed [L].
4. **Samsung QD-OLED does not exist at 42/48/83".** Every Samsung 42/48 OLED is LG Display WOLED: S90F, S90H, S95H and S85H [RTINGS, SamMobile, Tom's Guide, H].
5. **Sony sells no 42/48" OLED in 2025/2026.** The Bravia 8 II comes in 55/65 only (QD-OLED, the same panel as the S95F). The Bravia 8 comes in 55/65/77 (WOLED) [RTINGS titles/intros, H].

## Recommended score deltas vs the same model's 55" version

Scale assumption: points out of 100, in the same style as the example given ("-4"). Adjust to your rubric.

**LG**
- **LG G5 48: -6.** No Primary RGB Tandem, no MLA. Booster Max (1.5x vs B5) instead of Ultimate. 144 Hz vs 165 Hz. This is effectively a slightly boosted C5-class panel at G5 prices. (H)
- **LG G6 48: -4.** Booster Pro x2.1 vs Ultra x3.9. "Reflection Free" vs "Reflection Free Premium". Likely not Tandem 2.0 / Hyper Radiant. (H spec, M size of gap)
- **LG C5 48 / 42: -3.** No Brightness Booster, which LG explicitly excludes at 42/48. The panel is otherwise the same generation. (H)
- **LG C6 48: +2** (better than the C6 55). Tandem panel per RTINGS measurement, plus Booster. Glossy coating as on the other sizes. (M–H)
- **LG C6 42: -3.** No Booster; WOLED. (H)
- **LG B5 48 / B6 48: 0 to -1.** Same panel class as the 55; small-panel density gives slightly lower peak. (M)

**Samsung**
- **Samsung S95H 48: -5 to -6.** LGD WOLED instead of QD-OLED (lower colour volume, no QD). Keeps the matte Glare Free coating and 165 Hz, so do not penalise reflections. (M)
- **Samsung S90F 48 / 42: -4 vs a QD-OLED 55, or -1 vs a WOLED 55.** EU 55 units may be WOLED, so check the model suffix. (H for the 48 panel, L for the EU 55)
- **Samsung S90H 48: 0 (range -1 to +1).** US units are tandem (RTINGS). Samsung IE gives a lower HDR tier ("OLED HDR" vs the 55's "OLED HDR+"). Unresolved. (L)
- **Samsung S90H 42: -2.** Lower HDR tier than the 55, smaller speakers. (M)
- **Samsung S85H 48: 0.** WOLED at all sizes. (H)

**Other brands**
- **Philips OLED810 / OLED811 48: -2.** Lower brightness spec than the 55+. (M)
- **Philips OLED+911 48: -1.** Same tandem family; the smaller panel is likely somewhat dimmer. (L)
- **Panasonic Z90B 48: -1 to -2.** Unverified. (L)
- **Sony:** no 42–48" OLED in the current range. Remove from the 48" shortlist.

## 48" models that are essentially panel-equivalent

**Group A, 2025 LGD 48" WOLED evo (no tandem):**
- LG C5 48 (no booster).
- LG G5 48 (same class, but LG adds Booster Max processing: +1.5x vs B5).
- Samsung S90F 48 (LGD, 144 Hz).
- Philips OLED810 48 and OLED760 48.
- Panasonic Z90B 48 [L].
- The LG B5 48 is a step below (non-evo, 120 Hz).

Within this group, panel differences are small. Processing, heat management and coatings separate them: the G5 > C5 for brightness, and the Samsung has HDR10+ but no DV.

**Group B, 2026 LGD 48" tandem WOLED:**
- LG C6 48 (RTINGS-measured tandem).
- Samsung S90H 48 (RTINGS, US).
- Philips OLED+911 48 (official "Tandem WOLED").
- LG G6 48: probably the same panel. LG's own claim is x2.1, similar to the C6 48 rather than the G6 55. [L]
- Samsung S95H 48: LGD-made; probably the same panel [L]. Its Glare Free matte coating is a real differentiator.

Practical result: **at 48", the C6 48 is close to the G6 48, and possibly to the S90H 48 / S95H 48.** In 2026 the premium tier at 48" buys coating and processing, not a better panel. Score G6 48 vs C6 48 within about 1–2 points (G6: Booster Pro and Reflection Free; C6: same tandem). Score S95H 48 vs S90H 48 within about 1–2 points.

**Group C, conventional 2026 WOLED 48":** LG B6 48 and Samsung S85H 48.
